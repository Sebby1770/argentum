Argentum is an open-source derivatives analytics library and local research Studio for education, model exploration, and prototyping. Bring a last print — from a demo book, a public close, or your market-data endpoint — and it estimates the option, the swap, the bond, the CDS. Greeks, smiles, crash scenarios. Not a broker. Not a data provider or validated risk system. A research desk that runs on this machine.

What this site is

This is Argentum Studio: a window onto a Python derivatives library. Under the glass sit ISDA day counts, TARGET2, dual-curve swaps, SABR, Heston, barriers, lookbacks, CDS. Every number on this page was computed here, by those engines — not by a spreadsheet, not by a model in the cloud.

Most “pricing” sites are a Black–Scholes form and a hope. A desk needs the boring things to be right: 30E/360 on a February termination, OIS discounting of a Libor projection, implied vol that round-trips, a CDS that accrues on default. Argentum does that work, then lets you point it at a print you actually care about.

It will not send an order. It will not store your blotter in someone else’s basement. Connect a market and the last trade becomes spot. From there the question is the one a desk asks: what is this worth, and what happens if the print moves.

What it does

A quote comes in. The library treats it as the underlying. Four engines mark the same contract. You read a ticket, not a guess.

  1. 01 Your market

    Demo book, Stooq close, or a JSON URL from the venue you run.

  2. 02 Last print

    spot / last / price / close becomes S.

  3. 03 The engines

    Analytic, tree, finite difference, Monte Carlo — same inputs.

  4. 04 The ticket

    NPV, delta, vega, a smile, a crash PnL. On this machine.

How it can help

Explore a contract with a market print

Pull AAPL, or a ticker from your own matching engine, and the lab prices an option on that print — four engines, one contract, no copy-paste into Excel.

See the greeks, not just the premium

Delta, vega, theta, volga, an American value. Enough to decide whether a quote is tight, whether a hedge is sized, whether the smile is lying to you.

Stress the book before the tape does

The blotter bumps spot, vol, and rates through the same positions. A crash scenario is a button, not a spreadsheet you last updated in March.

Learn identities that do not drift

ATM 100 / 5% / 20% / 1y is 10.4506 because the formula says so, and the tests refuse to ship if it moves. Use it as a desk, or as a tutor that will not invent NaNs.

Stooq

Public close

Daily close from stooq.com. Delayed, free, good enough to walk a symbol through the desk.

Your venue

HTTP JSON

GET a URL with {symbol}. Polygon, Finnhub, IEX, Alpha Vantage, or the JSON you already emit. Localhost is welcome.

Feed

Offline SHA-256 book. Same ticker, same last, no network.

Put {symbol} in the path or query, {key} if the vendor wants the token in the URL. Argentum reads spot / last / price / close, Polygon results.p, Finnhub c, IEX latestPrice, or a bid/ask mid. Retries, a short cache, and a circuit breaker sit in front of the socket. Saved secrets show as ••••. No file://, no cloud-metadata hosts. Venue vol, rate, and div must be decimal fractions (for example 0.20 for 20%). The venue timestamp is displayed but Studio does not enforce a freshness policy for you.

    Shape of a quote

    Pull with a URL, or push from a matching engine after starting Studio with ARGENTUM_INGEST_TOKEN: POST /api/ingest with an X-Argentum-Ingest-Token header. A local mock is python examples/own_market.py --no-auth then http://127.0.0.1:8791/quote/{symbol}. Nested fields and vendor shapes both work.

    Your venue stays yours. A configured credential is sent only with the request to the endpoint you chose. Stooq is a public close, delayed, good for a walkthrough. The demo book is deterministic so the desk still works on a plane. This is a mark, not an order gateway.

    A session

    1. Connect a feed on this page. Demo is enough to see the shape of it.
    2. Pull a symbol. The last print becomes the spot — watch the tape above.
    3. Open the lab. It prices a call or put on that spot, four engines at once.
    4. If you run a book, send the same spots into scenarios and watch PnL.

    House rules

    01
    Evaluation date is an argument. Never a process global.
    02
    Discounting and forecasting are separate curves.
    03
    A market feed supplies spots. It does not replace the model.
    04
    Implied vol of a model price returns the input vol.
    05
    Domain errors raise. They do not return NaN.
    06
    A failing venue trips a circuit. A cached print may be served stale, never invented.
