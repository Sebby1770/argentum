What the numbers mean

The ATM Black–Scholes call with S=K=100, r=5%, σ=20%, T=1 is 10.45058357. That is not a fitted constant. It is S N(d1) − K e^{−rT} N(d2) with d1=0.35, d2=0.15.

Day counts

Act/365F, Act/360, Act/Act ISDA, 30U/360, 30E/360 ISDA. The 2003-11-01 / 2004-05-01 ISDA split is a unit test.

SABR

Hagan 2002 lognormal expansion, ATM and F≠K branches, z/x → 1 when ν=0. Shifted SABR is the same formula on F+s.

Heston

Fang–Oosterlee COS with the little-Heston-trap characteristic function so the complex logarithm stays principal-valued.

COS models

One Fang–Oosterlee engine prices Black–Scholes, Merton, Variance Gamma, NIG, Heston and Bates from their characteristic functions. Heston matches the published 5.785155435 to nine digits; VG matches 19.099354724.

SSVI surface

Gatheral–Jacquier SSVI with power-law φ. Fits keep θt non-decreasing and η(1+|ρ|) ≤ 2, γ ≤ ½, so the surface is free of static arbitrage; Dupire local vol follows in closed form.

Chain arbitrage

Every quote becomes a call price by parity, then: price bounds, verticals (slope between −DF and 0), butterflies (convexity) and calendars (total variance non-decreasing at fixed ln K/F).

American

Bjerksund–Stensland 2002 (a lower bound, within 1.5% of a fine tree) and Longstaff–Schwartz, whose reported price uses an exercise rule fitted on independent paths — a lower bound with an honest error bar.

Your market

Studio pulls a last print from a demo book, Stooq, or any HTTP JSON venue ({symbol} in the URL). Presets for Polygon, Finnhub, IEX, Alpha Vantage. Probe, watchlist, session tape, and authenticated POST /api/ingest so a matching engine can push ticks. Retries, cache, circuit breaker. That number is spot — not an order.

Full formula sheet: docs/THEORY.md in the repo. This studio is a local desk, not investment advice, and not a substitute for model validation.
