# Argentum brand-risk note

This is a launch warning, not legal advice or trademark clearance.

The bare name **Argentum** is already used by multiple software, finance, and
other businesses, including active Australian entities and an established
software company overseas. A preliminary name search also found Australian
trade-mark history involving the exact word in other classes. That creates
search-discovery, confusion, domain, and possible legal risk even though it
does not decide whether this project may use the name.

Before taking payments, buying ads, registering a company, or filing a mark:

1. commission a proper Australian and target-market clearance search;
2. decide whether to adopt a more distinctive compound brand;
3. check domains, package indexes, app stores, company names, and social handles;
4. document the goods/services and markets actually planned; and
5. get qualified advice before relying on availability.

The repository keeps its existing name because a rename is a product-owner
decision with migration costs. Nothing in the launch site says or implies that
the name is registered, exclusive, or cleared.
