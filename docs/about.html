Why Argentum

Many examples stop at a single Black–Scholes function. Argentum explores the less glamorous layers around it: calendars, day counts, schedules, curves, solver behaviour, portfolio aggregation, and data failure modes.

What it does not claim

It is not a broker, exchange, data entitlement, audited risk system, formal model-validation service, or source of investment advice.

How it can earn revenue

The code remains MIT licensed. Revenue can come from scoped setup and implementation guidance when a user values the maker's time and context.
