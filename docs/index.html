Model coverage

More than a one-line option formula

Argentum covers a broad research surface while keeping conventions visible: curves and day counts, equity and FX options, rates, credit, volatility models, numerical engines, and risk.

Equity, FX, exotics

European and American vanillas, barriers, lookbacks, Asians, quanto, multi-asset structures, trees, finite difference, and Monte Carlo.

Rates and credit

Yield-curve bootstrap, separate discount and forecast curves, swaps, caps, swaptions, bonds, hazard curves, and CDS.

Volatility and risk

SABR, SVI, Heston COS, local volatility, Greeks, DV01, VaR helpers, book aggregation, and parallel scenarios.
